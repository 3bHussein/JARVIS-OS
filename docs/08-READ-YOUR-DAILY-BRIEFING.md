# 08 — Read your daily briefing

1. Open **Today** after unlocking JARVIS. Recorded sleep, food and remaining calories stay above **Start here**.
2. Review up to three **Start here** rows. These are navigation suggestions from your recorded plan, not an AI importance score. They can point to overlapping times, today's next actual timed entry, due reminders, missing inputs or untimed notes. **Open** takes you to the relevant section without saving or completing anything.
3. Read the provenance line: sleep uses usable WHOOP or manual data; food uses your explicit cloud/file/manual selection. Unavailable inputs remain unknown, including when the selected cloud/file source cannot provide today's total. Zero is shown only when recorded.
4. Expand **Preview what JARVIS will read**, then use the existing **Read my briefing** action if you want speech. The spoken briefing includes missing-data statements and source labels. Existing device/network-voice consent still applies.

The list contains recorded entries only. No next appointment means no matching recorded appointment, not that you are free. Untimed notes are not calendar reservations. Completing a task, changing a source or saving a check-in still uses the original controls and confirmations. Source updates can change which inputs are usable; the list does not write records, call an AI provider, create appointments, recommend exact training loads or prescribe nutrition.
