# 06 — Use JARVIS online

Open https://jarvis-os-henna-sigma.vercel.app/ on your phone or computer. You do not need to download the project or start a local service for the online workspace.

## 1. Open your workspace

Click **Open your workspace**, enter your online password and unlock it. If you forgot it, expand **Forgot your online password?** and follow the email-code flow. Never share the code or password in chat.

## 2. Finish one setup step at a time

The small setup prompt shows the next missing step. **Continue setup** opens the relevant section; the full checklist stays under the expandable setup heading. You can navigate before finishing your profile.

Choose **encrypted cloud saving** explicitly when prompted if you want your profile, notes and manual check-ins retained. Missing values remain unknown; the checklist is not a health score or proof of connected integrations.

## 3. Use the five daily sections

| Section | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Today | See your recorded sleep/food, next appointments and recent notes |
| Planner | Add, edit, complete or review notes and timed commitments |
| Check-in | Record sleep hours and food calories for a selected date |
| Sources | Connect original fitness accounts or import a ProgressXX backup |
| You | Edit your profile, storage choice and portable backup controls |

These buttons stay visible while scrolling within your daily workspace. Subsections include **Back to today**. Your drafts stay in their existing section when switching between these sections; locking/reloading is a separate boundary.

## 4. Capture something quickly

On Today, enter text under **Capture it before you forget**. Leave the type as **Note**, or choose **Task** / **Workout**.

Notes can be untimed. Expand **Add a time or duration** if needed. Tasks and workouts reveal the scheduling fields and need a real time. Click **Review quick entry**, check the preview, then **Confirm save quick entry**. The preview alone does not save. Use Planner to manage saved entries.

## 5. Understand your sources

- Manual sleep and food stay labelled as manual records.
- ProgressXX supports saved cloud-row reads for the unlocked session and a separate backup import. Online Home defaults to cloud food; the visible source selector lets you choose manual input or an imported file deliberately. Manual entries are preserved. Missing cloud reads never silently use them.
- WHOOP reads saved records through your existing WHOOP Intelligence account after explicit session consent. No new vendor OAuth or vendor sync is started. See [07 — Connect fitness accounts](07-CONNECT-FITNESS-ACCOUNTS.md) for password/email-link instructions and one-hour session limits.
- Missing or outdated readings are not replaced with sample values. Food intake and WHOOP energy expenditure are different measurements.

## 6. Find apps and install the web app

Open **Projects** and use **Find your app** to filter the app list. Search does not trigger a connector read or change the original apps. App launch links open the existing applications.

In Settings, use **Install JARVIS** if your browser offers it, or the browser menu's **Install app / Add to Home Screen**. Online sign-in and cloud records need an internet connection. Installation does not make record editing available offline.

Use the language button for Arabic/English. Motion preferences include reduced or disabled animation. These device preferences are separate from saved personal records.
